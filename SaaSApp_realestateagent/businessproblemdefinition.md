Real Estate Intelligence Agent
Business Problem

Real estate stakeholders need an AI-powered assistant to support property evaluation, rental analysis, and tenant management. These activities require stakeholders to assess property information, analyze rental-related information, and manage tenant-related information. The Real Estate Intelligence Agent will provide AI assistance across these three areas to support more efficient and informed real estate decision-making.

Project Purpose

The purpose of the project is to develop a Real Estate Intelligence Agent that assists real estate stakeholders with property evaluation, rental analysis, and tenant management based on the information available to them.

Target Outcomes
Enable stakeholders to evaluate properties more efficiently using available property information.
Enable stakeholders to analyze rental information more effectively to support rental-related decisions.
Enable stakeholders to manage tenant information and activities more efficiently.
Improve the efficiency and quality of decision-making across property evaluation, rental analysis, and tenant management.
Required Functionalities

The Real Estate Intelligence Agent must provide the following functionalities:

Property Evaluation
Rental Analysis
Tenant Management
Defined Scope

The project scope is limited to:

Property evaluation
Rental analysis
Tenant management

No additional functionality is included within the defined scope.
